# 🚀 Final Year Project: AI-Enhanced Hiring Platform 🌟

Welcome to the repository for our groundbreaking Final Year Project (FYP)! Our industry-level web application is designed to revolutionize the hiring process by integrating advanced Artificial Intelligence technologies. Dive into the core functionalities that make our platform a must-have for modern recruiters and job seekers alike.

## Key Features

### 1. 📄 **Ranking Resumes**
Harness the power of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and, potentially in the future, a Large Language Model (LLM) controller. Our sophisticated algorithms evaluate and score CVs, identifying the top candidates for any job posting effortlessly. This feature ensures that recruiters have access to the best matches quickly and accurately.

### 2. 🔍 **Matching Job Postings to Candidate Profiles**
Utilize AI-driven insights to connect job postings with the most suitable candidate profiles. This function not only enhances visibility for employers but also ensures that candidates find their ideal job matches. Our platform’s AI capabilities allow for a broader reach and more effective job search experience.

## 🌐 How It Works

- **Resume Scoring**: Upload resumes, and our AI evaluates them based on a custom scoring function. It filters through numerous applications to spotlight top talents.
- **Job-Candidate Matching**: Enter job details, and our AI recommends the best candidates from the pool. This automated matching saves time and increases the efficiency of the hiring process.
